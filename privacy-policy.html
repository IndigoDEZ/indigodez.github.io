1. What the app does

Scan2Skin 3D: Avatar Creator makes a player skin (a small picture file that dresses your character in block-building games) from a live camera capture of you, or of someone who agrees to be scanned. All of the processing happens on your phone. The app works without an internet connection, except for optional ads and purchases.

2. What the app analyzes, on your phone only

  • Camera images of the person being scanned.
  • Depth data from your phone's depth sensor, if it has one.
  • Measurements the app works out from these: the shape and position of the face and its features (eyes, brows, mouth, glasses, hair), body proportions, and the colors of skin, hair, eyes and clothes.

The app uses these only to draw the skin. It does not use them to recognize, identify or match anyone, and it does not guess age, sex, gender, ethnicity, health or emotion. They are never used to train or improve any model, and they are never sent to us, to advertisers, or to anyone else.

3. How long photos are kept

Camera photos are held in the app's private storage on your phone only while a capture is open: at most 10 photos (a two-layer capture takes one set without a jacket and one with it), plus the walk-around frames while the best ones are chosen. They are deleted when you finish, when you cancel, before you start editing pixels, and, if the app closes unexpectedly, the next time it opens. Photos are never written to your photo library.

Depth data and face and body measurements exist only in the phone's memory while a skin is being made. They are never saved.

4. What the app keeps on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us, and none of it is included in cloud backups or in phone-to-phone transfers.

  • My skins. Each finished skin (in both arm widths, Classic and Slim), its name, dates, a color legend (which colors are skin, hair or clothes, so colors can be adjusted later) and whether its export is unlocked. For a skin with a raised hood, the app also keeps the same skin without the hood, so its "without the jacket" version keeps what the hood covered. My skins holds no photos and no measurements. It stays until you delete a skin, use "Delete all data", or uninstall the app. It holds at most 200 skins.
  • A draft of unsaved edits, one at a time, so pixel edits survive if the app closes. It is removed when you save or discard the edits.
  • A record that the scanning notice was accepted: the date, the version of the notice and the kind of confirmation. It contains no name and no image.
  • A record that the welcome summary was accepted: the date and its version.
  • No list of your phone's sensors. The app checks which sensors your phone has (for example a depth sensor) each time it opens, to choose the capture method. The result is not stored.

Skins you export or share (as PNG files or skin packs) are copies under your control. Where they go is up to you.

5. What we do not collect

The app never asks for your location, contacts, microphone, or access to read your photo library. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app contains no analytics and sends no crash reports. We do not use advertising identifiers.

6. Google Play Services for AR

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

On Android the app uses ARCore on your phone for motion tracking and depth during the walk-around capture. It does not use ARCore's cloud features (Cloud Anchors or Geospatial), and it never sends camera images anywhere.

7. Ads (free version only)

The free version shows a video ad only when you choose "watch an ad to export" to save or share a skin. There are no banners and no other ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request is child-directed and non-personalized, with the most restrictive content rating (G), and the app does not use an advertising ID.

When an ad is shown, AdMob receives information about your device and network connection that it needs to deliver the ad and to prevent fraud. Google handles this under its own privacy policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services). AdMob never receives your camera images, measurements or skins. If no ad can load, for example when you are offline, you can still export.

The Pro version has no ads, and the ad software is not started while Pro is active.

8. Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we receive only a confirmation that Pro was bought, never your payment details. "Restore purchases" uses your store account. We hold no account of our own.

9. Why the data is processed

  • To make the skins you ask for, on your phone: this is needed to provide the app to you.
  • To show a rewarded ad when you choose one, so the free version can export skins.
  • To comply with the law.

10. Who receives data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Nothing from your captures is shared with anyone. When you choose to watch an ad, Google AdMob receives device and network information as described in section 7. Google Play handles purchases. Nobody else receives data from the app unless the law requires it.

12. Your choices and rights

Because we receive nothing from your captures, you control your data directly in the app:

  • Delete skin removes a skin from My skins at once, together with any draft of edits to it.
  • Delete all data (in Settings) removes everything the app keeps: all saved skins, drafts, any photos still held, and the notice and welcome records. Skins you already exported or shared are not affected.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it keeps.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. You can contact us at z.farm.technologies@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days (45 days for requests under California law). Requests about ad data are answered with directions to Google. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data-protection authority.

13. Face data

This includes residents of Illinois, Texas, Washington and other places with biometric privacy laws. The app measures face geometry only in your phone's memory while making a skin, then discards it. We do not collect, store, sell, disclose or profit from face geometry, and it is never used to identify anyone.

14. Scanning other people

Only scan yourself or someone who agrees to be scanned. For a child, a parent or guardian should do the scan or agree to it first. The app asks you to confirm this before your first scan. It scans one person at a time and asks you to reframe if it sees more than one person. The background is removed on your phone before anything else is analyzed.

15. Children

Scan2Skin 3D: Avatar Creator is not specifically directed at children under 13, but it is built to be safe for them. We collect no personal data from anyone, there are no accounts, ads are child-directed and non-personalized, and there are no social features, chat or public sharing. We recommend that parents do the scan with their child. Contact us with any questions.

16. Security

Everything the app keeps stays in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. There is no server holding your data, so none of it can be exposed from us. If we ever learned of a problem affecting personal data, we would act promptly and inform the affected users and authorities where the law requires.

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date. For material changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
